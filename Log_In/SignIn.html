Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

During account registration, Bancheto De Bustos collects your fullname, username, email address, and assigned role. Your password is never stored directly by Bancheto De Bustos — it is securely hashed and managed by our authentication provider, Supabase.

2. Use of Information

Information you provide is used to authenticate your account and apply the correct, role-based access to the system (Owner, Manager, or Cashier).

3. Login Activity

Login date, time, username, role, and login status are recorded for security and monitoring purposes.

4. Data Storage

Account and business data — profiles, menu items, inventory, transactions, and login history — are stored in a secured database managed by Supabase, not in your browser. Your browser only keeps a temporary session token so you stay signed in; this token never contains your password and is cleared when you log out.

5. Data Retention

Account and activity records are retained for as long as your account remains active, or as needed for business, security, or legal recordkeeping. Login history and inventory logs are kept to support auditing and security monitoring.

6. Information Protection

Users should keep their account credentials private and secure, and should avoid sharing their login with anyone else.

7. Data Removal

Authorized administrators (Owners) may disable or remove accounts when appropriate. If you would like your personal information removed, please contact your system administrator using the Support details on this page.

8. Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary.
